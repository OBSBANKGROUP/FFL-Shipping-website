Warehousing & Distribution

Bonded and ambient storage, pick-and-pack and fulfilment from hubs beside the ports.

Storage that works as hard as your supply chain

We operate bonded and ambient warehousing at Umm Qasr port and our Baghdad logistics hub. Bonded storage lets imported goods be held under customs supervision without duty payment until ready for distribution — a significant working-capital advantage for importers and distributors.

Our facilities include racking systems, temperature-controlled zones for pharmaceutical and food-grade products, 24-hour security and CCTV, and a warehouse management system giving you real-time inventory visibility through our online portal.

Beyond storage we provide value-added services: labelling, repackaging, palletisation, pick-and-pack for e-commerce fulfilment, and consolidation of multiple SKUs into outbound shipments. We can deliver directly to your customers or to onward freight terminals.
