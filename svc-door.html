Door to Door

We collect from the sender's address and deliver to the receiver's door — ocean, air or road, customs handled, no handover gaps.

One partner from pickup to doorstep

Door-to-door shipping means you give us two addresses — collection and delivery — and we handle everything in between. That includes origin pickup, export documentation, freight (ocean, air or road), international transit, destination customs clearance and final-mile delivery right to the receiver's door.

Most forwarders pass cargo to a different agent at destination, creating handover gaps where things go wrong. Fast Forward Logistics uses owned offices in Iraq and vetted long-term partners at destination to ensure one point of accountability throughout the journey.

Door-to-door is priced all-in: one quote covering freight, origin and destination handling, and customs duties where applicable. No surprise invoices. No strangers at the other end.

Available on ocean (FCL and LCL), air freight and road freight. Can be combined with our warehousing service for multi-stop or inventory-holding arrangements.
