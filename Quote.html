Quote questions

Is the on-screen estimate the final price?

No — it's an indicative range to give you a feel for cost. Your firm all-in quote arrives by email after we check live carrier rates and space for your exact shipment.

What if I don't know the exact weight or volume?

Give your best estimate. We'll confirm the chargeable weight when we quote — for air freight that's the greater of actual and volumetric weight.

Do you quote door-to-door?

Yes. Add pickup and delivery addresses in the notes and we'll include inland haulage on both ends.

Which lanes do you cover?

From Iraq to the USA, across Europe, and worldwide through our agent network — ocean, air and road.
