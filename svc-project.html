Project & Heavy Cargo

Out-of-gauge, breakbulk and charter solutions engineered for oversized and complex industrial moves.

No load too large, no move too complex

Fast Forward Logistics manages out-of-gauge, breakbulk and heavy-lift project cargo for the oil and gas, construction, energy and manufacturing sectors. Iraq's energy sector demands regular movement of drilling equipment, transformers, pressure vessels and steel structures — cargo that does not fit a standard container.

Every project move begins with a detailed scope: weight, dimensions, centre of gravity, transport route survey, and permit identification at each transit point. We then select the optimal vessel — heavy-lift ship, multipurpose carrier or RoRo — and organise port craneage and specialist road transport from origin to port and port to site.

Our project team has executed moves for oil-field operators and infrastructure contractors in Iraq and across the Gulf, consistently delivering on schedule even for multi-component projects spanning several months.
