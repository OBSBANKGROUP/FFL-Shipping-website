Ocean Freight

FCL and LCL from Umm Qasr to the USA, Europe and worldwide — reliable schedules, competitive rates.

Sea freight built for Iraq's trade lanes

We operate FCL and LCL services from Umm Qasr — Iraq's primary deep-water gateway — to all major destinations in the United States, Europe and beyond. Direct carrier contracts with MSC, CMA CGM, Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd mean real space, competitive rates and weekly sailings locked in at booking.

Our Basra team handles the full origin chain: export documentation, container stuffing, terminal gate-in and verified bill of lading issuance. From there every port of call is tracked live to destination delivery.

Transit times: 26–40 days US Coasts · 16–26 days Europe · 18–32 days Gulf and Asia. Our control tower flags any deviation before it becomes a delay.
