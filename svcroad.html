Road & Rail

Cross-border trucking via Turkey into Europe, and intermodal rail links bridging the first and last mile.

Land corridors out of Iraq

For Turkey, the Caucasus, Central Asia and Europe, road and rail can be significantly faster and more economical than ocean routing through the Gulf. We operate regular truck services from Baghdad and Erbil via the Ibrahim Khalil / Habur border crossing into Turkey, and onward through the TIR network to European destinations.

Road transit: Iraq to Turkey 3–6 days · Turkey to major European cities 5–10 days FTL or 8–16 days groupage. For inland cargo we provide haulage to Umm Qasr for ocean connections and airport runs for air shipments.

All cross-border moves are handled with full TIR Carnet documentation, and our team manages the customs formalities at each border point so your driver never waits longer than necessary.
