Our story

Built in Baghdad, trusted worldwide

Fast Forward Logistics started in 2009 with a single question from Iraqi exporters: why is moving cargo out of the country so hard? Paperwork stalled at the border, carriers went quiet for weeks, and no one could say where a shipment actually was.

We set out to fix that — first with our own customs desk in Baghdad, then with direct carrier contracts out of Umm Qasr, and finally with the live tracking you see on this site. Today we move ocean, air and road freight for date exporters, manufacturers, pharmacies and traders, connecting them to buyers across the USA, Europe and the wider world.

The promise hasn't changed since day one: one coordinator who knows your account, one reference number that follows your cargo, and no surprises between pickup and proof of delivery.

Our journey

15 years of moving cargo forward

2009

Founded in Basra

Started with one truck and a single lane — Umm Qasr port to Baghdad. The vision: honest freight, no surprises.

2013

First international lane

Signed our first ocean carrier contract with MSC. Weekly FCL departures from Umm Qasr to Rotterdam launched.

2016

Baghdad & Erbil offices open

Expanded to three offices across Iraq. Air freight desk launched from BGW and EBL international airports.

2019

USA corridor established

Direct weekly sailings to New York and Houston. First Iraqi forwarder with a dedicated US trade lane coordinator.

2022

Live tracking platform launched

Built our own real-time customer portal. Shipment visibility milestone by milestone, from booking to delivery.

2024

140 countries served

Completed our 50,000th international shipment. Bonded warehousing opened adjacent to Umm Qasr port.
