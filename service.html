Good to know

Freight questions, answered

What Incoterms do you work with?

All of them — EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF, DAP and DDP. If you're not sure which fits, tell us where your responsibility should end and we'll recommend the right term and price it accordingly.

What documents do I need to export from Iraq?

Typically a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and any product-specific certificates. Our in-house team prepares and checks the paperwork so nothing is missing at the border.

Do you handle door-to-door, or just port-to-port?

Both. We can collect from your warehouse in Iraq and deliver to the final address abroad, or handle any segment in between — you choose the scope.

Can you move temperature-sensitive or hazardous cargo?

Yes. We arrange reefer containers and cool-chain air for perishables and pharma, and we're set up to handle declared dangerous goods with the correct documentation.

How do I get a firm price?

Use the quote page for an instant indicative estimate; a coordinator then confirms carrier space and sends a firm all-in quote within one business day.
