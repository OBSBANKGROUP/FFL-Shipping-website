Air Freight

Priority uplift from Baghdad and Erbil to Europe, the USA and worldwide — door-to-door speed for time-critical cargo.

When days matter, not weeks

Fast Forward Logistics moves time-critical, high-value and temperature-sensitive cargo by air from Baghdad International (BGW) and Erbil International (EBL). We work with Lufthansa Cargo, Emirates SkyCargo and Turkish Cargo to offer daily options to Frankfurt, Dubai, Istanbul and onward to every global hub.

Air freight is priced on chargeable weight — the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight (m³ × 167). We calculate this precisely at booking so you always know the full cost before cargo moves.

Transit times: 2–4 days to Europe · 4–7 days to the USA · within a week to most global destinations. For pharmaceutical, perishable and high-value goods we arrange dedicated cool-chain handling and priority loading.
